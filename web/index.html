C600 Studio 0.2.1

The full puzzle remains in mechanics in every display mode. The web client uses a custom renderer; it is not a fork of HSC. The included native launcher uses the actual MPUlt executable and its controls.

Left-drag rotates the 3D view. Right-drag or Alt-drag rotates in 4D. Ctrl-click focuses a cell. Shift-click selects a physical piece. The seven grip controls cover every local tetrahedral rotation. Right-click a grip for its inverse.

Macros require a legal witness and full collateral check. Protection applies at macro endpoints. Use File → Save checkpoint before changing tools or importing records.
