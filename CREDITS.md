# Credits

**Andrey Astrelin created Magic Puzzle Ultimate**, including its original multidimensional puzzle simulation, geometry conventions and renderer. C600 Studio extends that work with its own workbench and state-management layer. Andrey's [original project page](https://superliminal.com/andrey/mpu/) permits use of the source with clear, obvious author credit.

The public [cutelyaware/MPUlt](https://github.com/cutelyaware/MPUlt) source and the reviewed [ivan216/MPUlt](https://github.com/ivan216/MPUlt/tree/df8881b97b3862ecd6c5f2e943802e656cbb72a5) fork helped document the native interfaces. Their MIT notice names **Copyright (c) 2018 Melinda Green** and is retained verbatim in `licenses/MPUlt-MIT.txt`; that notice is not replaced by this acknowledgement.

Thanks to **Nan Ma** for historical 600-cell solving work. His [2012 account](https://archive.hypercubing.xyz/messages/2439.html) describes the simplified puzzle. It is not a claim that his log solves this full shallow-cut model; no personal solve log or private strategy document is included.

The keybinding presentation was informed by [MC7D-KB](https://github.com/ivan216/mc7d-kb), and filter design by [Hyperspeedcube](https://ajfarkas.dev/projects/hyperspeedcube/). Their frontend source is not included. These acknowledgements do not imply endorsement by the upstream authors.
